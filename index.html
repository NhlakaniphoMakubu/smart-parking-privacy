Privacy Policy for Smart Parking

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Introduction

Smart Parking (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is a mobile parking management application operated by Nhlakanipho Makubu (“the Developer”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using Smart Parking, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Information We Collect

When you create an account or use the App, we collect:

  • Account information: your full name, email address, and password (passwords are securely hashed and never stored in plain text)
  • Vehicle information: vehicle registration number, make, model, and colour that you provide
  • Usage information: parking reservations, entry and exit times, parking history, and token balance
  • Authentication data: if you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile information from Google

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, or any other information from your device.

How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Enable you to reserve parking bays
  • Operate the parking gate access system
  • Display your parking history and account balance
  • Communicate with you about your account or the service
  • Improve the App and fix technical issues

How Your Information Is Stored

Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore and Realtime Database), which is operated by Google LLC. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to your data is restricted to your authenticated account only.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared only:

  • With Google Firebase as our data storage provider
  • If required by law or legal process
  • To protect the rights and safety of users or the public

Your Rights

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct or update your information (via the Profile section of the App)
  • Delete your account and associated data by contacting us
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, we will remove your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it.

Children’s Privacy

Smart Parking is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
